# NUTRIX — Product Roadmap

NUTRIX aims to make everyday nutrition information easier to understand through food intelligence, nutrition tracking, personalized planning, and AI-assisted guidance.

This roadmap outlines potential development priorities. Items listed as planned are not necessarily implemented or released.

## Project Priorities

- Make food and nutrition information easier to understand.
- Support informed everyday food choices.
- Provide convenient food logging and nutrition tracking.
- Offer personalized dietary planning tools.
- Build a polished, accessible Android experience.
- Handle personal information responsibly.

## Phase 1 — Core Android Application

**Focus:** Establish the application foundation and core navigation.

Areas of work:
- Android application structure using Kotlin.
- Shared visual design components and theming.
- Navigation between major application screens.
- Food-related data models and nutrition calculations.
- Local data persistence.
- Initial food and nutrition experience.

## Phase 2 — Food Intelligence

**Focus:** Improve food discovery and nutritional awareness.

Potential improvements:
- Expand packaged-food lookup.
- Improve supported food scanning workflows.
- Broaden Indian food and regional dish coverage.
- Improve serving-size calculations.
- Present calorie and macronutrient information more clearly.
- Explain health-rating criteria and data limitations.
- Provide useful alternatives when food identification fails.

## Phase 3 — Food History and Progress

**Focus:** Make nutrition tracking more useful over time.

Potential improvements:
- Improve food history organization.
- Provide daily and weekly nutrition summaries.
- Improve serving quantity editing.
- Add clearer progress visualizations.
- Support convenient correction of food records.
- Provide useful summaries of eating patterns.

## Phase 4 — Personalized Diet Planning

**Focus:** Adapt general dietary guidance to user-selected goals.

Potential improvements:
- Support weight gain, weight loss, muscle gain, maintenance, and balanced nutrition goals.
- Improve weekly meal planning.
- Add dietary preference options.
- Support meal substitutions.
- Improve grocery planning.
- Clearly communicate the limitations of estimated nutritional requirements.

## Phase 5 — Hydration and Daily Routines

**Focus:** Make everyday nutrition habits easier to maintain.

Potential improvements:
- Customizable hydration targets.
- Flexible meal and water reminders.
- Improved reminder scheduling.
- Better daily routine summaries.
- More control over notification frequency and preferences.

## Phase 6 — AI Nutrition Assistance

**Focus:** Improve access to understandable food-related guidance.

Potential improvements:
- More useful food and nutrition conversations.
- Context-aware suggestions based on user-selected goals.
- Better handling of missing or uncertain food information.
- Improved error handling for unavailable AI services.
- Clear explanations of AI-generated estimates.
- Evaluation of local and remote AI approaches.

AI assistance should remain informational and should not replace professional medical or dietetic advice.

## Phase 7 — Localization and Accessibility

**Focus:** Make NUTRIX easier to use for more people.

Potential improvements:
- Improve English, Telugu, and Hindi language coverage.
- Improve accessibility and readable text layouts.
- Support different screen sizes and font scaling.
- Improve navigation clarity.
- Refine dark-theme consistency and visual feedback.

## Phase 8 — Privacy, Reliability, and Maintainability

**Focus:** Strengthen the application as it evolves.

Potential improvements:
- Review Android permissions.
- Improve error handling and recovery.
- Review API credential management.
- Document data storage and deletion behavior.
- Improve offline access where feasible.
- Maintain dependency and security documentation.
- Improve automated test coverage.
- Document architecture and developer workflows.

## Long-Term Opportunities

Potential future directions include:

- Broader food database coverage.
- More transparent nutrition-rating explanations.
- Better personalized food recommendations.
- More detailed nutrition analytics.
- Improved offline-first experiences.
- Additional language and accessibility support.
- Integrations with supported health and fitness data sources, subject to appropriate permissions and privacy safeguards.

## How Priorities May Change

Development priorities may change based on user feedback, technical constraints, data quality, privacy requirements, and available resources.

This roadmap is directional rather than a promise of delivery dates. Feature availability should be confirmed from the current source code and published releases.

## Suggestions and Feedback

Suggestions for improvements are welcome. Please use the repository's issue-tracking facilities where available and include a clear description of the proposed improvement.
